# The app walk with real content (#200)

Walked on 2026-11-13 at `main` (36d94d6), against the local database filled by `pnpm content:import`: 13,108 Kanji, 219,040 Words, 1,115 Grammar Points (all Drafts), 236,204 Sentences. Desktop Chrome at 1280 wide, light, the Shu look. I signed up as a new Learner through the real form and the Mailpit link, then used each screen. Scripts and raw screenshots are not committed; the screenshots cited here are in [`screenshots/walk-200/`](screenshots/walk-200).

## Verdict

A new Learner cannot study. Two things stop them, and everything else on this page is smaller.

1. **Learn, the Library's item panel and Listening answer 500.** Each one loads the whole content graph.
2. **The real content has no Examples.** Reviews, the Learn quiz and Cram all ask about an Example, and there are none.

Behind those, Grammar is empty at every level (no Grammar Point is approved yet), and Listening and Reading have nothing to show. Those two are known and sit under #191.

## Findings

### 1. The whole content graph is loaded per request

`learningPath.pathItems()` calls `loadPath`, which reads every Study Item, Word, Sentence and link, about 700,000 rows. `withOverrides` then puts every record id into one `inArray(override.recordId, …)`, and drizzle builds that query with `result.params.push(...query.params)` in `mergeQueries`, which throws `RangeError: Maximum call stack size exceeded` past roughly 120,000 arguments. I reproduced it with a longer stack trace: the last app frame is `withOverrides` at `core/content-graph/src/postgres.ts:174`.

What the Learner sees:

- `GET /api/learn/batch` and `?level=N3` → 500. Learn shows the raw text "Internal Server Error" ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/learn-500.png)). Today still offers "Learn 5 new →" ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/today.png)).
- `GET /api/browse/items/:id` → 500. The Library panel says "The Library could not load this." ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/library-item-500.png)).
- `GET /api/listening/practice/options` → 500. The page shows "No N5 clips yet" instead of an error ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/listening.png)).
- Today takes 3.8 to 4 s on every visit; the level pages, Reviews and the Library take about 0.5 s.

The fixture hides this because it has a few hundred rows. Even without the overflow, loading the graph per request will not scale; `pathItems` needs to read only what the path strategy uses.

### 2. The real content has no Examples

`content_graph.example` has 0 rows. Examples are generated by the `example-sentence` kind in content-generation, which needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, and #191 does not list them. Without Examples:

- Cram says "No Example to practise yet for 七, 語." and "0 of 0 right" ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/cram-no-examples.png)).
- Reviews and the Learn quiz have nothing to ask. I could not check this live behind finding 1.

This is the real blocker once finding 1 is fixed. It needs a human decision on spend, like #191.

### 3. Two bugs seen earlier and not fixed

- **`/learn?level=n3` shows N5.** `SourceChooser` starts its radio from `sourceOf(tracks)`, the Learner's saved active Tracks (N5 by default), not from the `?level=` filter that `LearnScreen` passes to `api.batch(level)`. The batch is for N3 and the chooser says N5. Found by reading `slices/learn/web/SourceChooser.tsx` and `LearnScreen.tsx`; I could not see it on screen because the page 500s.
- **A wrong Kanji reading answer shows the Kanji, not the reading.** Not reproduced: it needs an Example, which this database does not have (finding 2).

### 4. Placement can show the wrong level's wall, and then fail to save

Open Placement, press Words, Grammar, N3, Kanji, then N5 and Kanji quickly. The wall shows the 367 N3 Kanji under "All of N5 Kanji" and "79 of 746 Known". "All of N5 Kanji" then ticks 367, and Save returns `400 {"message":"a ticked item is not on this level"}` with "Placement could not save your ticks." ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/placement-stale-wall.png)). A stale response replaces the wall of the level the Learner has since chosen.

Also on this screen: the level and kind chips do not show which one is on ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/placement-words.png)); saving 1,652 Words takes more than 1.5 s with no sign it is working; "No N5 Grammar yet." has no way forward.

### 5. Grammar is empty everywhere

All 1,115 Grammar Points are Drafts, so none is approved: the Library says "No Grammar at N5 yet" ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/library-grammar.png)), Placement says "No N5 Grammar yet.", and the N3 page says "Grammar 0 of 0" ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/level-n3.png)). The level pages count 746 items at N5 and 2,019 at N3 with no Grammar. Covered by #191's grammar part and the open question about N4 to N1 grammar on #185.

### 6. N5 Words show spellings nobody writes

The Word walls show the first JMdict form, which for words that are usually written in kana is a rare kanji spelling: 然うして (そうして), 如何 (いかが), 幾つ (いくつ), 何方 (どなた and どちら, twice), 有る (ある), 良く (よく), 瓩, 頁. Full-width digits and letters come through too: ７日, ４日 and Ｙシャツ ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/library-words.png)). A beginner would learn the wrong spelling. JMdict marks these forms as usually-kana; the display form should follow that.

### 7. Reading, Reviews and Today empty states

- Reading with no stories: the text "No stories yet. Finish a Learn batch…" touches the top edge of its card and the button sits tight under it ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/reading-empty.png)). It also points at "Learn a batch", which 500s today.
- Reviews with nothing due is good: "Clear until Nov 21, 09:36" and three doors ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/reviews-nothing-due.png)).

### 8. Smaller things

- **Settings at 1280** ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/settings.png)): the signed-in email runs into the Devices pane, and the Devices pane prints the raw user-agent string, clipped at the card edge, rather than "Chrome on Windows".
- **Logged out, `/learn`** shows "log in first" in plain text with no link ([screenshot](screenshots/walk-200/learn-logged-out.png)). Cram, Placement and the other session screens probably do the same.
- **Search**: `/api/browse/search?q=taberu` finds nothing (no romaji), and results carry `level: null` for items that have a level. `q=water` lists お水 and the radical 氵 before 水.
- **The command menu** matches screen names only, so typing 食べ shows "Nothing goes by that name." That matches #207 as drawn; say so if it should find Study Items.
