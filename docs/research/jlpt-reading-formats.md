# JLPT reading section: passage types and lengths

Researched 2026-11-05 for issue #31, to set the style and length targets for Batch Texts ([batch-text.md](../batch-text.md)). Lengths are Japanese characters (字). Only the N1 and N2 figures come from official JLPT material. The lower-level figures come from third-party study guides and are approximate.

## Passage types

| Type | Content | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| 内容理解（短文） Short | Memo, notice, email, short explanation of daily life or work | N5–N1 |
| 内容理解（中文） Mid-size | Explanation, essay, diary; tests reasons and cause and effect | N5–N1 |
| 内容理解（長文） Long | Essay or commentary; tests the gist and the author's thinking | N3–N1 |
| 統合理解 Integrated | Two or more texts compared | N2, N1 |
| 主張理解（長文） Thematic | Abstract, logical commentary; tests the main claim | N2, N1 |
| 情報検索 Information retrieval | Ads, brochures, notices, tables, business documents; find specific facts | N5–N1 |

## Approximate lengths

| Level | 短文 | 中文 | 長文 / 主張理解 | 統合理解 | 情報検索 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | ~200 | ~500 | ~1000 | ~600 total | ~700 | Official guidebook |
| N2 | ~200 | ~500 | ~900 | ~600 total | ~700 | Official N2 guideline (主張理解); rest assumed close to N1 |
| N3 | 100–200 | 300–400 | ~550 (estimate) | — | ~600 (estimate) | Third-party |
| N4 | ~100 (estimate) | ~400 (estimate) | — | — | notice or table | Third-party, interpolated |
| N5 | ~80 | ~250 | — | — | notice or table | Third-party |

Figures marked "estimate" are our interpolation and are the starting config values, not exam facts.
