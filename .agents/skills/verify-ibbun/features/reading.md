# reading

Reading Passages and Batch Text episodes (`slices/reading`, issue #94). A Learner reads the stories generated for them after Learn batches, newest first (issue #204), taps Words for lookups and switches the translation on when they want it. Practice Text passages (`slices/reading/practice`, issue #111) are generated by Learners on a Cleared Level.

## Sub-features

- **Episode list** (`/reading`): the Learner's newest Batch Text leads in a card: "Your newest story", its title, "Episode N · level" and the accent button **Read**. The earlier ones are a plain list under "Earlier stories", newest first across all levels, each a title link with "Episode N · level". A text whose passage is not approved yet is left out. The link **Practice Texts** is always there. With none: "No stories yet. Finish a Learn batch and your first one arrives." and the accent link **Learn a batch** to `/learn`.
- **Text screen** (`/reading/texts/:id`, `/reading/passages/:id`): title, "Episode N · level" for a Batch Text, and three switches in the header (group "Reader", each a button with `aria-pressed`): **Furigana** (on), **Translation** (off), **Words** (off). Sentences in order with Words as buttons, the text in the middle of the screen. Words of the Batch Text's batch carry `data-in-batch="true"`.
- **Translation switch**: the English of every Sentence that has one shows under it while **Translation** is on, and is hidden otherwise. There is no per-Sentence button.
- **Word list**: hidden at first. **Words** brings in a side list (`complementary` "Words") on the right, below the text on a phone: each Word of the text once, in reading order, tappable for a lookup, the batch's Words marked "New". **Furigana** off removes every `rt`, in the text and in the list.
- **Lookup**: tapping a Word shows its form, reading and meanings in a status region and logs a `lookup` Review (`POST /api/reading/lookups`). It never introduces the Word or feeds the scheduler.
- **Furigana** (with the switch on) per the Learner's `furigana` preference: On = every Word with kanji, Off = none, Mixed (default) = only Words still New (unlinked Words count as New).
- **Practice texts** (`/reading/practice`, linked from `/reading`): heading "Practice texts". With no Cleared Level (every unsuspended Kanji of a level Known) it says "Practice texts open once you know every kanji of a JLPT level." and shows no form. Otherwise a Generate form with selects only (Level = cleared levels, Passage type, Length, Topic from a fixed list) and no text input. "Generate" posts the choices, waits up to 20 s, then opens `/reading/practice/:id` (the same reader as for Batch Texts) or says "Your text is still being made. It will appear in your library when it is ready." Below, "Your library" lists the Learner's own Practice Texts ("Still being made" until approved). At 5 in a rolling 24 h it says "Try again tomorrow."
- **API**: `GET /api/reading/episodes`, `GET /api/reading/batch-texts/:id`, `GET /api/reading/passages/:id`, `POST /api/reading/lookups`, `GET /api/reading/practice/options`, `POST /api/reading/practice/texts`, `GET /api/reading/practice/texts`, `GET /api/reading/practice/texts/:id` (owner only).

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, then "Reading" in the nav (`/reading`). Press **Read** on the newest story, or pick an earlier one.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- The Compose stack, API and web app are up (the `verify-ibbun` skill does this) and the browser has a Learner session.
- A fresh Learner has no Batch Texts, so the page shows "No stories yet." with **Learn a batch**. To exercise the text screen, seed an approved Reading Passage (with Sentences, tokens and `sentence_uses_word` links) and a `progress` Batch Text row for the Learner pointing at it. `/reading/passages/<id>` needs only the passage.

Steps:

1. `page.goto("/reading")`; expect the heading "Reading", then the **Read** link under "Your newest story" or "No stories yet."
2. Press **Read**; expect Furigana pressed, Translation and Words not. Press **Translation** and expect the English under the Sentences; press **Words** and expect the "Words" side list.
3. Click a Word button; expect the lookup status region, and a `lookup` entry in the Learner's Review log.
4. Change the `furigana` preference (On, Off, Mixed), reload, and expect `rt` elements to follow the rule.

5. For Practice Texts, seed Known progress for every unsuspended Kanji of N5 (otherwise the page shows the locked message), open `/reading/practice`, press Generate, and expect either the text screen or the library notice; after 5 texts expect "Try again tomorrow.".

The specs are `slices/reading/tests/reading.spec.ts` (the newest story or the empty state is accepted) and `slices/reading/tests/practice.spec.ts` (locked state or the form).
- **One level.** Open `/reading?level=n5`: only the N5 stories are shown (`GET /api/reading/episodes?level=N5`). Click **All** in the "Level filter": every level's stories are back.

## Gotchas

- Furigana readings come from the stored Sudachi tokens (katakana, shown as hiragana). A Sentence without tokens renders as one plain run.
- Only Words linked by `sentence_uses_word` are tappable; particles and unlinked Words are plain text.
- Not built: highlighting earlier items (the Batch Text record stores only the batch's items), Grammar Point highlighting, reading from the end of Learn (#learn slice).
- Practice Texts need the worker running with an LLM key to turn a request into a text; without it every request ends in the library as "Still being made". The shared Learner-triggered token budget is not shown to the Learner: when it is spent the text simply stays in the library until the budget resets.
