# learn

The `learn` slice (`slices/learn`): the start page with its source chooser, a batch of New Study Items, a card with two sides for each, the quiz that must be answered fully correctly, and the Tally that ends the batch, with the Batch Text offered from it. Learn has no daily limit: a Learner takes as many batches as they like. Passing the quiz appends a Review of `kind` `learn` per item, so the items go into Reviews. **Mark as Known** appends a `manual` Review with `source` `learn-skip`. A Listening Clip is taught here too, as a three-step lesson (`slices/learn/listening`, issue #105).

## Sub-features

- `learn-clip-lesson` teaches a Listening Clip that `learning-path.next()` puts in the batch (a Learner with an active `listening_clip` Track), in its place among the lessons, in three steps. **Listen**: "Play"/"Replay" and a "Slow (0.75×)" checkbox, always, whatever Exam Mode says. **Read**: "Read it" shows each line with its speaker (男/女), the text, a collapsible translation and links to the clip's Words and Grammar Points (`/browse/items/<id>`). **Quiz**: "Start quiz" asks the clip's Example (dictation, or a comprehension question) on a fresh play. The clip is not in the batch quiz: its own quiz step is the one.
- `learn-clip-quiz` honours Exam Mode (Learner preference `examMode`): the question is shown before the audio, "Play" works once ("Played once" after), no Replay and no slow checkbox. Otherwise the question appears after the first Play. Every try goes to `POST /api/learn/clip/answer`, which grades on the server. A miss shows "Not quite" and the right option (comprehension), and **Try again** asks on another fresh play; a try that is not an answer keeps the question open. A pass appends one `learn` Review for the clip with `exampleId`, the facet (`dictation` or `comprehension`), earlier tries in `attempts[]` and `listening: {replays, slow, examMode}` (in Exam Mode `{0, false, true}`), and counts as one item learned on the Tally.

- `learn-source` is the one chooser on the start page at `/learn`: a **Level** radio group (N5 to N1, Unlevelled) and the checkboxes **Kanji**, **Words** and **Grammar**. Learn draws from one level at a time. A change saves at once through `PUT /api/learn/tracks` (one Track per ticked type at that level) and loads the batch again. A level chosen while no type is ticked ticks all three. The levels read as one strip with the chosen one in ink; each type is a switch whose dot fills with the accent when it is on. The chooser opens on the level of the Learner's first Active Track. Listening Tracks are not in it and are kept as they were.
- `learn-start` shows the batch before it starts under **Next up**: one ruled line of the Study Items, each a large glyph with its type under it in small caps ("Word", "Kanji", "Grammar Point", "Listening Clip"), no reading and no meaning. **Start** begins it. An empty batch reads "Nothing new is left at this level. Choose another level or type above, or keep what you learned fresh." with the link **Start Reviews** to `/reviews`.
- `learn-batch` takes the batch from `learning-path.next()`: the Learner's `batchSize`, every time. Nothing limits how many a day (#189). Pulled-in dependencies come on top. `GET /api/learn/batch` returns it with the lessons and the quiz questions.
- `learn-lesson` teaches one Study Item at a time on a card with two sides. The session header counts "2 / 5" against the batch's fixed size. The front shows the item alone with **Not yet** and **I know this**. Either turns the card in place: "You said: …", then reading, meanings, details, Japanese Explanation and one Example, with **Next** (**Start quiz** on the last) and **Mark as Known**. The answer on the front changes nothing by itself. Every other Kanji of a batch, from the first, leads with its Example instead, the Kanji marked in it; the back adds the Kanji's reading over the mark and the translation. Words and Grammar Points always show alone first.
- `learn-quiz` asks each item through its first approved Example: a cloze for a Word or Grammar Point, a reading and a meaning facet for a Kanji. The Learner's try is graded in the browser with `grade()`. Every answer field carries its name above it ("Answer", or "Reading in this sentence" and "Meaning of the kanji"). After **Check** the result sits under a rule, one line per facet, a missed one in the error colour. A miss shows the answer and is asked again later; the quiz ends when every item has been answered right. The same try rules as Reviews apply: an empty try, leftover Latin letters or an alternate's nudge are not graded and are kept as `attempts`.
- `learn-record` sends the correct answers to `POST /api/learn/complete`. The API grades them again from the stored answer data, and stores nothing unless every answer passes (422). Then it appends one `learn` Review per item with `exampleId`, per-facet input and outcome, `attempts[]` and `timeToAnswerMs`.
- `learn-skip` records **Mark as Known** through `POST /api/learn/skip` and keeps the item out of the quiz. The item keeps its place in the count and is listed as "Known" on the Tally.
- `learn-tally` ends every batch, Learn now included: a large "N learned", the batch's items each marked "Learned" or "Known", **Keep going** (the next batch, enabled once it is loaded; with nothing left it opens the start page), **Read the story** when the batch has a Batch Text, and **Done for now** to Today.
- `learn-now` (`slices/learn/learn-now`) opens `/learn?now=<study item id>`, which is where Browse's **Learn now** button goes. `GET /api/learn/now/:id` returns the item and its pulled-in dependencies from `learning-path.forItem()` as a batch, locked or not. The same cards, quiz and Tally run; the screen sends `now: true` to `/complete` and `/skip`, so the Reviews carry `detail.source = 'learn-now'`. The item is introduced, so its Track skips it later. An item with nothing to teach by is a `State` under 新, "This item cannot be taught yet", with **Learn something else** to `/learn`. A failed load, on Learn or Learn now, is an alert "Not loaded · Learn did not load" with the server's message and **Try again**, which loads again.
- `learn-batch-text` asks for the batch's Batch Text when **Start** is pressed: `POST /api/learn/batch-text` with the batch's Study Item IDs enqueues `batch-text.generate` (level = the batch's hardest, Unlevelled hardest of all) and answers `{enqueued}`. It enqueues nothing, and answers `{enqueued:false}`, when the Learner already has 5 Batch Texts in the last 24 h or this batch already has its text.
- `learn-reading` is the reading screen behind **Read the story** on the Tally (offered only when a text was requested and at least one item was learned; never opened by itself). It asks `GET /api/learn/batch-text?ids=` every second for up to 20 s. While the passage is not approved the screen reads "Preparing your story…"; after 20 s "Your story is still being prepared. Read it later from Reading." with a link to `/reading`. Once approved it shows the Sentences: batch Words bold and underlined (`data-highlight="batch"`), earlier Words dotted-underlined (`data-highlight="earlier"`), furigana by the Learner's `furigana` setting, each translation hidden behind **Show translation**. Tapping a Word shows a lookup and logs a `lookup` Review (`POST /api/learn/lookups`). **Done** / **Skip reading** returns to the Tally, which no longer offers the story; Reviews are unaffected.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Log in, pick **Learn** in the nav, or open `http://localhost:5173/learn`. The Today screen's **Learn 5 new** button goes there too.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- `verify.sh doctor` reports every line `ok`; the N5 fixture is loaded (`content-graph-fixture.md`) and a verified Learner is logged in (`account.md`).
- For a clean run use a Learner with no Reviews.

- **Start page.** Open `/learn`. `shot /learn learn/start` shows **Level** on N5 with Kanji, Words and Grammar ticked, the **Next up** line and **Start**. No line about today. Untick **Words** and the cards change. Pick **N4**: the same types stay ticked and the cards are N4's.
- **Lesson.** Press **Start**. The header reads "1 / N" beside the screen's name. The card shows the item alone, or a Kanji marked in its Example. Press **Not yet**: the card turns in place, reads "You said: not yet" and shows the facts. **Next** moves on; the last card offers **Start quiz**.
- **Quiz miss.** Answer a question wrong and press **Check**. The status reads "Not quite" with the accepted answer; press **Next**. The "N left" count does not drop and the same question comes round again. Nothing is in `progress.reviews` yet.
- **Quiz pass.** Answer every question right. The Tally reads "N learned" with the batch listed. Check: `psql "$DATABASE_URL" -c "select kind, passed, detail->>'exampleId' from progress.reviews where learner_id = '<id>' order by seq"` shows one `learn | t | <example id>` row per item. Open `/reviews` after the first interval and the items are due.
- **Keep going.** On the Tally press **Keep going**: the next batch starts at "1 / N". Do it three times in a row. Each batch is full; no screen mentions a limit.
- **Mark as Known.** Turn a card and press **Mark as Known**. The count moves on, the quiz does not ask the item, the Tally lists it as "Known", and a `manual | t` row with `detail->>'source' = 'learn-skip'` appears.
- **Forged pass.** `curl -s -X POST -b <session cookie> -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"answers":[{"studyItemId":"<id>","exampleId":"<example>","facets":[{"facet":"cloze","input":"zzz"}]}]}' http://localhost:3000/api/learn/complete` prints a 422 message and `progress.reviews` gains no row.
- **Learn now.** Open a locked item in Browse (`/browse`) and press **Learn now**; the URL is `/learn?now=<id>` and the header reads "1 / N" with no start page. Pass the quiz: the Tally shows. Check: `psql "$DATABASE_URL" -c "select kind, passed, detail->>'source' from progress.reviews where learner_id = '<id>' order by seq"` shows `learn | t | learn-now` rows. `curl -s -b <cookie> http://localhost:3000/api/learn/now/<id>` returns the batch (404 for an unknown id, 400 for a malformed one).
- **Batch Text hand-off.** Start a batch with the worker stopped and read the queue: `aws sqs receive-message --endpoint-url http://localhost:9324 --queue-url "$JOBS_QUEUE_URL" --visibility-timeout 0` shows one `batch-text.generate` message with the batch's `itemIds`. Pass the quiz and press **Read the story** on the Tally: the screen shows "Preparing your story…" and then the story once the worker has generated and approved it, or the "still being prepared" note after 20 s. Tap a Word: the lookup panel appears and `progress.reviews` gains a `lookup` row. Start a sixth batch within 24 h: no new job and no **Read the story**.
- **Spec.** `pnpm exec playwright test --project=learner slices/learn/tests` passes.
- **One level.** Open `/learn?level=n4`. The header shows the `navigation` "Level filter" with **N4** current. The batch holds only N4 items of the active Tracks (`GET /api/learn/batch?level=N4`), even while N5 is unfinished. Click **All**: the address loses `?level=` and the batch is the usual one.

## Gotchas

- The reading screen needs a running worker with `content-generation` and `content-approval` plus an LLM key to produce a text; without them it always ends on the "still being prepared" note, which is the expected degraded path.

- The `learner` Playwright project exists only once `slices/account/register.ts` does.
- The batch is not stored. Reloading `/learn` before the quiz is passed returns the same items, because nothing is introduced until the quiz passes or **Mark as Known** is pressed.
- Learn now Reviews are `learn` Reviews with `source: learn-now`. Reviews and the Learning Path treat the item as introduced.
- `learner.preferences.daily_budget` is still a column, nullable and unread. Nothing writes it.
- A Word in the batch pulls its Kanji into the same batch, so a batch can be longer than `batchSize`.
- The default Active Tracks already include `N5:listening_clip`, so a new Learner meets a clip in the batch as soon as the Content Graph holds an approved clip with audio at N5 (no screen changes Listening Tracks yet; a Learner who changed them can be reset with `learningPath.setActiveTracks`). The N5 fixture has no clips: seed one first, or the batch simply has none. A clip's linked Words and Kanji come first as plain lessons, ahead of the clip.
- The clip lesson shows its text in the Read step; the quiz step's own text stays hidden until the Learner answers or asks again.

## Keys

- Lesson: **1** is Not yet, **2** is I know this, **Enter** is Next or Start quiz. Quiz: the first answer field has the cursor, **Enter** is Check and then Next. A Listening Clip lesson takes **Enter** for Read it and Start quiz; its comprehension choices are **1** to **4**. **Enter** is also Start on the start page and Keep going on the Tally.
- A key never runs while the Learner types in a field (Enter excepted), while a dialog is open, or with Ctrl, Alt or ⌘ held. Each key shows as a small `kbd` mark on its button, hidden on touch screens, and the button carries `aria-keyshortcuts`.
- Check: on a lesson card press 2, then Enter; the next card shows. In the quiz type the answer, press Enter twice; the next question shows with the cursor in its field.
